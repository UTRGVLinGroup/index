How to Join the Lin Research Group 

  • High school students: High school students are welcome to join our group in the summer through the UTRGV High Scholar program. 
  • Undergraduate students: We are always looking for new undergraduate students. If you are interested in either pursuing research for credit (CHEM4201/4202), volunteering for research, or being supported through the Engaged Scholar (ESAA) award/Scholarly Undergraduate-Based Interdisciplinary Research (SUBIR) award, please send me an email. 
  • Graduate students: A description of the graduate student position pursuing M. Sc. degree can be found here.
